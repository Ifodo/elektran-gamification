# Difficulty Levels Explained

## What Do Difficulty Levels Mean?

The difficulty levels in "Build the Deal Stack" indicate the **complexity** of the transaction and how **challenging** it is to get the correct sequence and avoid risks.

---

## 🟢 Beginner-Friendly ⚡

**What it means:**
- **Simpler transaction** with straightforward steps
- **Fewer prerequisites** - Most cards can be placed in any reasonable order
- **Clear mandatory requirements** - Easy to identify what's needed
- **Forgiving timeline** - More time relative to the number of steps
- **Lower risk** - Fewer opportunities to make critical mistakes

**Best for:**
- First-time users learning the system
- Understanding basic property transaction flow
- Building confidence before tackling complex deals

**Example:** Residential Purchase (Owner-Occupier)
- Standard home buying process
- Clear verification steps
- 45-day timeline for 10 mandatory steps

---

## 🟡 Intermediate ⚡⚡

**What it means:**
- **More complex transaction** with specialized requirements
- **More prerequisites** - Card sequencing matters more
- **Additional risk factors** - More ways to make costly mistakes
- **Tighter timeline** OR **More steps** to manage
- **Specialized knowledge** - Requires understanding of specific property types

**Best for:**
- Users who've completed beginner scenarios
- Learning about investment properties
- Understanding different transaction types

**Examples:**
- **Off-Plan Investment**: 180 days but with milestone payments and developer risk
- **Land Acquisition**: 60 days with community clearances and survey requirements

---

## 🔴 Advanced ⚡⚡⚡

**What it means:**
- **Highly complex transaction** with many moving parts
- **Strict prerequisites** - Correct sequencing is critical
- **Multiple risk vectors** - Tenant issues, income verification, compliance
- **High-value stakes** - Larger financial commitment
- **Professional-level** - Requires understanding of commercial real estate

**Best for:**
- Experienced users who've mastered simpler scenarios
- Learning about commercial transactions
- Preparing for real-world complex deals

**Example:** Commercial Property Acquisition
- ₦320M office complex purchase
- Tenant lease review, income verification, market analysis
- 90 days for 9-11 critical steps
- Multiple points where errors can derail the deal

---

## How Difficulty Affects Scoring

### 1. **Mandatory Card Count**
- **Beginner**: 8-10 mandatory cards
- **Intermediate**: 5-8 mandatory cards (but more specific)
- **Advanced**: 9-11 mandatory cards with dependencies

### 2. **Time Discipline**
- **Beginner**: Timeline accommodates all cards comfortably
- **Intermediate**: Timeline requires careful selection of optional cards
- **Advanced**: Timeline is tight—every card counts

### 3. **Sequencing Complexity**
- **Beginner**: Fewer prerequisite chains
- **Intermediate**: Some cards depend on 1-2 others
- **Advanced**: Multiple interdependencies

### 4. **Risk Penalty Severity**
- **Beginner**: Risky shortcuts deduct 20-30 points
- **Intermediate**: Risky shortcuts deduct 30-40 points
- **Advanced**: Risky shortcuts deduct 40-50 points

---

## Real-World Context

### Beginner-Friendly (Residential)
**Real-world scenario:** You're buying your first home in Lagos. The seller has all documents ready, and you just need to follow the standard verification process. This is the most common transaction type in Nigeria.

**Learning focus:** Basic due diligence, legal verification, timeline management

---

### Intermediate (Off-Plan/Land)
**Real-world scenario:** You're investing in an unfinished property or raw land. The process involves more specialized steps like developer vetting (off-plan) or community clearances (land). Higher return potential, but more risk.

**Learning focus:** Investment risk management, specialized verification, long-term planning

---

### Advanced (Commercial)
**Real-world scenario:** You're acquiring an income-generating office building with existing tenants. You must verify rental income, assess tenant stability, review lease terms, and ensure code compliance. This is a professional-level transaction.

**Learning focus:** Income analysis, tenant assessment, commercial valuation, complex negotiations

---

## Progression Recommendation

### Start Here:
1. **🏠 Residential Purchase** (Beginner-Friendly)
   - Get a Transaction Readiness Score (TRS) of 80+ before moving on
   - Learn the basic flow and terminology

### Then Try:
2. **🏗️ Off-Plan Investment** (Intermediate)
   - Apply your knowledge to a longer, investment-focused deal
   - Learn about milestone payments and escrow

3. **🌾 Land Acquisition** (Intermediate)
   - Understand land-specific risks and verification
   - Learn about community clearances and surveys

### Master With:
4. **🏢 Commercial Property** (Advanced)
   - Put all your knowledge together
   - Handle the most complex transaction type

## Tips for Each Level

### For Beginner Scenarios:
- ✅ Focus on **completing all mandatory cards**
- ✅ Don't rush—you have plenty of time
- ✅ Avoid risky shortcuts (red cards)
- ✅ Read card descriptions carefully

### For Intermediate Scenarios:
- ✅ Pay attention to **card prerequisites** (what must come before)
- ✅ Balance **time vs completeness** (not every optional card is worth it)
- ✅ Understand the specific risks (developer failure, land disputes)
- ✅ Plan your sequence before dragging cards

### For Advanced Scenarios:
- ✅ **Map out the full sequence** before starting
- ✅ Double-check **all prerequisites**
- ✅ Every risky shortcut can fail the deal—**avoid them entirely**
- ✅ Time is critical—**no room for red herrings**
